# FinSight Design System
**Version**: 1.0  
**Purpose**: Consistent styling across all logged-in screens  
**Target**: Indian users (Mumbai-based entrepreneur - Arjun Mehta)  
**Date**: October 2026

---

## Visual Identity

### Colors
- **Primary**: Deep Teal Green `#2F6F63` (trustworthy fintech)
- **Highlight**: Mint Green `#9BE7D3` (accent, active states)
- **Page Background**: Off-white `#F6F7F9`
- **Card Background**: White `#FFFFFF`
- **Income/Positive**: Green
- **Expenses/Over-budget**: Red
- **Warnings**: Amber

### Typography
- **Font Family**: Inter
- **Text Contrast**: Must meet WCAG AA (small text must be dark enough)

### Cards & Shadows
- **Border Radius**: 16px (rounded corners)
- **Shadow**: Very soft shadow
- **Background**: White cards on light off-white page background

---

## Currency & Number Format
- **Symbol**: ₹ (Indian Rupee)
- **Format**: Indian number system (e.g., ₹8,42,850)
